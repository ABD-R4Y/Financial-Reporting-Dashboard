# Financial & Operational Analytics Dashboards

A collection of custom interactive financial, risk, and operational dashboards designed to transform complex datasets into clear, actionable executive insights.

---

## Featured Dashboard Projects

### 1. APEX ALM — Banking & Asset-Liability Management Dashboard
An executive command center designed for banking operations, risk assessment, and financial compliance tracking.

* **Key Focus:** Asset-Liability Management (ALM), Basel III Capital Adequacy, Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR), and Stress Testing.
* **Core Capabilities:**
  * **Risk & Capital Compliance:** Tracks Total Assets (PKR 285.5B), Mark-to-Market valuation, Risk Scores, and highlights critical SBP Capital Adequacy Floor breaches (10.67% vs 11.5% floor).
  * **Scenario Engine & Stress Testing:** Interactive controls for yield curve shifts (+130 bps), credit spread shocks, and FX stress simulation.
  * **Portfolio Analytics:** Multi-series value trend tracking, bond rating distribution (AAA to BB), and duration/DV01 sensitivity analysis across government and corporate holdings.
  * **Audit & Anomalies:** Real-time red flag alerts for concentration risk and policy limits.

### 2. Bloom & Oud — Studio & Operations Dashboard
A modern commercial operations dashboard designed to monitor revenue streams, order fulfillment, and multi-location performance.

* **Key Focus:** Financial reporting, supply chain inventory, and sales channel breakdown.
* **Core Capabilities:**
  * **Revenue & Order Tracking:** Real-time visibility into monthly revenue (AED 286,540), fulfilled orders, average order value (AOV), and retention metrics.
  * **Channel & Category Breakdown:** Sales attribution across channels (Instagram, Website, WhatsApp, Walk-in) and category share (Anniversaries, Corporate, Weddings, Subscriptions).
  * **Operations & Inventory Watch:** Active delivery schedules with VIP tagging and automated stock warnings for cold storage inventory management.
  * **Multi-Location Insights:** Comparative monthly revenue breakdown across flagship studios in Downtown Dubai, Palm Jumeirah, and DIFC.
